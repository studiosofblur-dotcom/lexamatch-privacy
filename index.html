LexaMatch for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

LexaMatch is provided by Studios of Blur, based in India ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play LexaMatch, use optional Google sign-in and cloud saves, view advertisements, make purchases, or contact us. It also covers this policy website.

Privacy contact: studiosofblur@gmail.com. Studios of Blur monitors this inbox and manually handles verified account and data-deletion requests.

At a glance

  • You can play without signing in. Advertising and other SDK processing may still occur while playing as a guest.
  • Google sign-in creates or accesses a LexaMatch account through Firebase Authentication and enables automatic cloud-save synchronization.
  • Cloud saves include progress, coins, banner-removal status, reward records, and purchase transaction identifiers.
  • The game uses Google/Firebase, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Unity IAP, and Google Play services.
  • Signing out, uninstalling, or resetting local progress does not automatically delete your account or cloud save.

1. Information handled

Account and sign-in information

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase Authentication process sign-in credentials and basic account information, which can include your name, email address, profile image, Google account identifier, and Firebase user identifier. LexaMatch uses your display name in its interface and your Firebase user identifier to associate your cloud save with your account. Our game code does not request your Google password or access Gmail messages or contacts. Authentication tokens are passed to Firebase for sign-in; the Firebase SDK manages authentication sessions.

Progress and cloud saves

The game stores level identifiers and star results, your coin balance, whether banner ads have been removed, identifiers of claimed level rewards, and processed purchase transaction IDs. When signed in, it synchronizes this data to Cloud Firestore under your Firebase user identifier. Cloud records also include a schema version, save revision, integrity hash, and update timestamp.

On your device, LexaMatch stores game progress, settings, account-binding and synchronization information. It also keeps a local recovery copy when a cloud save replaces a local save. Local recovery copies are not a separate cloud backup.

Purchases

Google Play processes payments, with Unity IAP connecting the game to the store. The game handles product identifiers, store prices, purchase status, and transaction information to grant purchased content and prevent duplicate grants. Transaction identifiers are included in local and cloud saves. Studios of Blur does not receive your full payment-card number or card security code through the game. Google handles payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising, device information, and diagnostics

LexaMatch uses Unity LevelPlay ad mediation and Unity Ads for banner and optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising SDKs can process advertising and other device or app identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device model, operating system, language, app version, network information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. These services use information to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, detect fraud, and, where enabled and permitted, personalize ads. Actual processing depends on the SDK, network configuration, device settings, region, and applicable consent requirements.

Removing banner ads does not necessarily disable all SDK processing. Rewarded advertisements remain optional, and watching them uses the relevant advertising services. LexaMatch does not request GPS-based precise location for gameplay.

Analytics

LexaMatch includes Google Analytics for Firebase. It can automatically collect app-instance identifiers, app interactions and session events, device and operating-system information, app version, language, approximate geographic information, and certain purchase or advertising events. Advertising identifiers may be processed where available and permitted by configuration and device settings. These services help measure app usage and performance. The game does not currently provide a dedicated in-app analytics opt-out switch.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary personal documents. This static website does not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or a contact form of its own. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the site. Email is handled through our Gmail account.

4. Retention and deletion

Local data: game data and recovery files remain in app storage until replaced or removed. Clearing the app's storage normally removes local app data. Device or operating-system backups may be managed separately through your device settings.

Account and cloud data: the current service does not automatically expire accounts or cloud saves after inactivity. They remain stored until removed through a verified deletion request or another administrative action. Signing out or uninstalling does not remove these server-side records. See how to request account and data deletion.

Other records: support messages are retained as needed to handle the request and related disputes or obligations. Analytics, advertising, security, and store records have separate retention settings or provider policies; deleting a LexaMatch cloud save does not itself erase those services' records. Where required, we will handle applicable requests concerning data under our control and explain any lawful retention exception.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution reasons. If an exception applies to a deletion request, we will explain the relevant categories and reason. There is no single retention period that applies to every service listed in this policy.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Play without Google sign-in, or sign out to stop subsequent account-based cloud synchronization. Existing cloud data is not deleted by sign-out.
  • Choose whether to watch rewarded ads and whether to make purchases.
  • Use available Android advertising-ID and ad-privacy settings. These do not necessarily stop all advertising, analytics, or security processing.
  • Review the privacy controls described by Google and Unity in the links above.
  • Contact studiosofblur@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing, or to raise a privacy concern.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, obtain a portable copy of information, or complain to an appropriate data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Rights and legal exceptions vary by jurisdiction. For account deletion, follow the dedicated request instructions.

6. Teens and younger users

LexaMatch is intended for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. This does not mean everyone aged 13 or older can consent independently in every country. Different rules can apply to users under 18, including in India; where required, processing of a minor's information must involve appropriate parent or guardian permission and protections. If you are below the applicable age to use these services independently, ask a parent or guardian before using account, advertising, or purchase features.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without required permission, contact studiosofblur@gmail.com so we can investigate and address the information. Please identify LexaMatch and provide only what is reasonably necessary to locate the account.

We use measures such as encrypted local save files and authenticated access controls for cloud saves. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. These measures are not a guarantee against loss or unauthorized access.

Studios of Blur is based in India. Google, Unity, and hosting or other service providers may process information in other countries. Applicable protections and transfer requirements depend on the service, location, and law. Provider privacy information is linked above.
