LexaMatch account & data deletion

Last updated: 30 October 2026

You can request deletion of your LexaMatch account and associated cloud save without having the game installed. Studios of Blur monitors studiosofblur@gmail.com and manually handles verified account and data-deletion requests. This page does not delete data automatically.

How to send a request

  1. Email studiosofblur@gmail.com with the subject LexaMatch account deletion request.
  2. Where possible, send it from the Google email address you used to sign in to LexaMatch. Say that you want your LexaMatch account and its associated cloud-save data deleted.
  3. If you no longer have access to that email address, explain this in your message. We may ask for reasonable additional information to verify ownership. A display name alone is not sufficient proof.

Compose deletion request

The button opens your email app; it does not send a message or delete anything automatically. If it does not work, copy the address and subject above into any email service.

Never send your password, one-time codes, Google authentication tokens, or payment-card details. Do not post account details in a public GitHub issue.

What the request covers

After verifying ownership, we will remove the LexaMatch Firebase Authentication account and its associated Cloud Firestore save. The cloud save contains:

  • Level progress and star results.
  • Coin balance and banner-removal status.
  • Claimed level-reward records and processed purchase transaction identifiers.
  • Associated save revisions, hashes, timestamps, and account association.

This deletes the LexaMatch account, not your Google account. It can remove access to cloud-backed progress, coins, and purchase benefits. Deletion is not a refund; Google Play handles payment records and refund requests separately.

Local data and other services

A server-side request cannot remotely remove files from your devices. To remove device data, sign out of LexaMatch and use Android Settings to clear the app's storage on each device. This includes the game's local save and recovery copy. Manage any operating-system backups separately. Signing out, clearing storage, or uninstalling by itself does not delete your cloud account.

Avoid signing back in while a deletion request is being processed. Signing in again after deletion can create a new LexaMatch account; future gameplay may create new data.

Google Play transaction records and advertising or analytics information are handled separately from the Firebase account and cloud-save document. Deleting those two Firebase records does not automatically erase every provider record. Tell us if your request also concerns other information so we can handle data under our control and explain the relevant provider privacy options. See the provider links in our privacy policy.

Verification, timing, and any retained information

We may need to verify ownership before deleting personal data. We will respond through email and process verified requests within applicable legal deadlines. We will confirm completion or explain any issue or lawful exception; this page does not promise immediate or automatic deletion.

A request record or limited information may be retained where necessary for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute-resolution obligations. Where an exception applies, we will explain the affected categories, reason, and relevant retention period or criteria. Providers may retain separate records under their own policies. The current game does not automatically delete inactive accounts after a fixed period.

You can also email a request concerning specific personal data without asking to delete the whole account. Describe what you want removed; we will explain whether this is possible and what effect it would have on your game data.
